Flow Music Player is an ultra-lightweight, audiophile-grade offline music player for Android, forked from the open-source Lune project (GPLv3).

Key Features:
- Sample-accurate Gapless playback with next-track queue pre-buffering
- ReplayGain 1.0/2.0, EBU R128, SoundCheck & real-time BS.1770 LUFS volume normalization
- Conflict-free Multi-Artist & Multi-Genre indexing with customizable Whitelist and title feat. extraction
- Extended codec & container playback: ALAC, AIFF, CAF, DSD (DSF/DFF), APE, WavPack, MKV/MKA, MP4, M4A, WebM, Stereo & 5.1/7.1 Opus, E-AC3, AC-3, AC-4, DTS, WMA, FLAC, WAV, OGG, AAC, and MP3
- Adaptive Material 3 UI for compact phones, landscape mode, foldables, and tablets
- Responsive home-screen widget, synchronized LRC & embedded lyrics, 10-band Equalizer, Bass Boost, Spatial Audio, Reverb, and Dynamics Processing
- Ultra-Saver and Light Mode profiles engineered for modest and older devices

Privacy First:
- Zero Internet Access: Does not request the INTERNET permission
- Zero Trackers, Zero Ads, 100% Offline local playback
- Open Source under GPLv3 (fork of https://github.com/MrDemonc/Lune)
